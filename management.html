Management

  • Introduction to Management: Learn what management means and why it is important in any organization.
  • Planning: Understand how to set goals and make plans to achieve them.
  • Organizing: Arrange resources like people, tools, and tasks for better results.
  • Leadership: Learn how to guide and motivate people in a team or company.
  • Communication: Understand how clear communication helps in good teamwork and decision-making.
  • Time Management: Use time wisely to complete tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Project Management: Learn how to plan and handle big projects using proper methods and tools.
  • Decision Making: Make good choices using facts and analysis.
